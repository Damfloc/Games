📻 How to Play Wavelength ✖

Wavelength is a social guessing game of telepathy and spectrums.

1. The Psychic: One player secretly sees where the colored target wedge is located on the dial (from Left to Right).
2. The Clue: The Psychic gives a clue that represents where the target falls on the spectrum (e.g. if the spectrum is Cold <-> Hot and the target is 80% to the right, clue might be "Coffee fresh out of the kettle").
3. The Guess: The Guessers discuss live and turn the red dial needle to where they think the clue lands!
4. The Reveal & Scoring:
  • 🎯 Bullseye (Center): 4 Points
  • 🔶 Inner Ring: 3 Points
  • 🔴 Outer Ring: 2 Points
  • ⚫ Miss: 0 Points
