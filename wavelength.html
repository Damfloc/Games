📻 How to Play Wavelength

Wavelength is a party game of mind reading, empathy, and hilarious debates where two or more players try to read each other's minds across a spectrum.

1. The Setup

One player is chosen as the Psychic. The psychic secretly sees where the hidden target wedge is positioned on the dial from 0% (Left) to 100% (Right).

2. The Clue

The Psychic gives a single clue that exists at that exact position on the spectrum. For example, on the spectrum Cold ◀──▶ Hot, a target around 85% might prompt the clue "Fresh black coffee".

3. Calibrating The Dial

Teammates discuss and drag the red needle to where they think the clue lands. Fine-tune using the stepper buttons, then lock in your guess!

4. Scoring

  • 🎯 Bullseye (Center): 4 Points
  • 🔥 Very Close (Adjacent): 3 Points
  • ⚡ Outer Edge: 2 Points
  • ❌ Outside Target: 0 Points
