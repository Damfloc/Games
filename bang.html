📦 Modular Expansions
🌵 Dodge City Expansion (+40 Cards & Green Delayed Actions)
Adds Green equipment cards, 15 new characters, 8-player scaling with 2 Renegades, and discard-to-play mechanics.
☀️ High Noon Expansion (13 Global Event Cards)
Sheriff reveals an event card each round with town-wide rules (Curse, Shootout, Ghost Town) ending in the deadly High Noon showdown.
🎪 Wild West Show Expansion (10 Stagecoach Events & 8 Characters)
Playing Stagecoach or Wells Fargo draws crazy new wild rules. The final card overrides victory to "Last Gunslinger Standing".
💰 Gold Rush Expansion (+24 Equipment Deck & Gold Economy)
Earn gold nuggets on damage & healing. Purchase Brown instant & Black in-play equipment from the 3-card Saloon Shop. Adds 8 characters.
👻 Shadow-Gunslingers Variant (Eliminated Players Take Turns)
Dead players retain gold nuggets and take turns with 0 LP (cannot heal), discarding at end of turn. Renegades dynamically join the losing team.
